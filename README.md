## The Arcanist

The Arcanist is a modern take on the toolbox mage, the wizard with an answer for every situation. Where many classes in Draw Steel draw their power from what they do to the battlefield, the Arcanist brings perhaps the greatest tool of all: options.

- **Flash Recall**: a special maneuver that swaps a prepared ability for another one you know, even in the middle of a battle. You can't swap another ability of the same type or cost until the fight is over.
- **Magical Schools**: choose two of ten, like Abjuration, Graviturgy or Illusion.
- **Grimoires**: small packages, each with a spell-like feature for outside of combat and one simple maneuver.
- **Arcane Paths**: the Orb, the Staff, the Sword, the Tome or the Wand, each shaping your wizard into its own playstyle.

## Features

**Ready to Play**: The class and every school and path at 1st level, with their abilities and grimoires, ready to pick during hero creation or drag onto a sheet.

**Rules at Hand**: The Preview as a journal in the Compendium Packs tab, laid out like the book, for checking a rule mid-session.

**Component Checks**: You can only prepare an ability once you hold its Component Prerequisite.

**Automated Features**: Ponder, Destructive Dominion and other features happen on their own at the right moment.

**Preparation Tracking**: Every ability you know stays on your sheet, but only the prepared ones can be used. Change them during a respite and the sheet keeps track.

**One-Click Flash Recall**: Press Prepare with Flash Recall on its chat message and your sheet shows every ability you can swap in right now, so switching mid-combat is a breeze!

## The Enhanced Experience

The Arcanist works on its own, but it's built to shine alongside my other Draw Steel modules. For the ultimate Arcanist experience, I strongly recommend installing them both!

- **[Draw Steel: Combat Tools](https://github.com/TheGenieOfTheCode/draw-steel-combat-tools)** unlocks the Arcanist's [Enhanced Compendium](https://github.com/TheGenieOfTheCode/draw-steel-arcanist-preview/wiki/Enhanced-Compendium): its abilities and features apply their effects straight from the chat message, from potencies and recurring damage to forced movement and more.
- **[Draw Steel: Triggers](https://github.com/TheGenieOfTheCode/draw-steel-triggers)** reminds you the moment a prepared triggered action, a school's focus gain or a class feature applies, and only reminds you about prepared abilities.

[What's Automated](https://github.com/TheGenieOfTheCode/draw-steel-arcanist-preview/wiki/Whats-Automated) lists everything the module handles, with and without them.
